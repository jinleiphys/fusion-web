Beta decay

Along a line of fixed mass number the nuclear masses form a valley. A nucleus on its slope turns a neutron into a proton, or a proton into a neutron, and steps down; the energy of the step is shared between the electron (or positron) and an antineutrino (or neutrino), so the electron comes out with a continuous spectrum.

What is drawn: one column per nucleus with an AME2020 mass, for the 17 mass numbers around the parent's and, along each, the nuclei within a few steps of its lightest isobar, so the columns fill a trench along the valley, standing on the (N, Z) plane (N to the right, Z away from you). Its height is its mass excess above the lightest nucleus of the same mass number A, so every isobar is a cut through the valley and the valley floor is at height zero. The vertical scale is set per nucleus: 10 units for the largest of the parent's own height, the measured heights on its isobar up to four steps either side of the floor, and 2 MeV; heights are linear up to 10.5 units and compressed above (those columns are drawn paler and are not to scale). Colour: the ground-state decay of NUBASE2020 (gold stable, blue β−, coral β+/EC, violet α, grey other); thin grey columns rest on estimated masses. The amber line joins the parent's isobar; the arrows are the steps down to the floor. A β− or EC step drops by exactly Q (the height difference), a β+ step by QEC, of which 2mec2 goes into the positron and the electron left in the atom. In each drawn event the energies are sampled from the computed spectrum, momentum is conserved exactly, and the arrows are the three momenta to one scale (the recoil carries a momentum comparable to the leptons' but almost no energy); the flight time is not to scale. Directions are isotropic and independent (the electron-neutrino angular correlation is left out), mirrored into the upper half so they stay above the columns. Materials, lights and glow are decoration.

Model, exactly
Allowed shape: dN/dW ∝ F(Z, W) p W (W0 − W)2, W the total electron (positron) energy and p its momentum in units of mec2 and mec, W0 = 1 + E0/mec2, Z the charge of the daughter nucleus. Recoil is left out of the phase space. E0 = Qβ− for β−, QEC − 2mec2 for β+ (atomic mass differences). Fermi function F = F0L0, both as written by Hayen et al., Rev. Mod. Phys. 90, 015008 (2018) (LaTeX source of arXiv:1709.07530 read). F0 = 4(2pR)2(γ−1) eπy |Γ(γ + iy)|2 / Γ(1 + 2γ)2, γ = √(1 − (αZ)2), y = ±αZW/p (+ electrons, − positrons), the point-charge Dirac solution cut off at R. In this convention the factor (1 + γ)/2 of the other common form, 2(1 + γ)(2pR)2(γ−1)..., sits in L0, the finite-size factor for a uniformly charged sphere of radius R: their Eq. for L0 with Wilkinson's coefficients (their two tables, electrons and positrons), stated accurate to 10−4 for p ≤ 45 and Z ≤ 60 and used beyond Z = 60 with a warning. R = 1.2 A1/3 fm for every nucleus (Hayen et al. take R from the measured charge radius). Not included: the diffuse-charge correction U, screening, atomic exchange and mismatch, recoil terms, the nuclear shape factor C, radiative corrections. The other choices: F0 alone, the nonrelativistic 2πy/(1 − e−2πy), and F = 1. f = ∫1W0 F p W (W0 − W)2 dW, by composite Gauss-Legendre in p; against a 30-digit mpmath evaluation (with the L0 coefficients read separately from the LaTeX source) it agrees to 8×10−14 (Z = 1 to 100, E0 = 0.01 to 20 MeV, both signs), F0 to 1.1×10−13 and L0 to 3×10−14. log ft for a β− emitter: the ground-state f times T½/BR, with BR the whole β− branch of NUBASE2020. The ground-state branch BRgs is at most BR, so the ground-state partial half-life T½/BRgs is at least T½/BR, and at the same ground-state f the true ground-state log ft is at least the number shown: a lower bound, not a measured log ft, equal to it when the whole branch goes to the ground state. For β+ the same is done with the e+ branch where NUBASE2020 gives it separately from EC (a value, or an upper limit, which still gives a lower bound); the electron-capture rate is not computed on this page. Branches NUBASE2020 marks as estimated (#) get no log ft. EC: the neutrino carries QEC minus the binding energy of the captured electron, which is neglected here.
Masses and Q: AME2020, Wang et al., Chin. Phys. C 45, 030003 (2021). Half-lives, Jπ, branches: NUBASE2020, Kondev et al., Chin. Phys. C 45, 030001 (2021). Superallowed transitions: J. C. Hardy and I. S. Towner, Phys. Rev. C 102, 045501 (2020), Tables I, IX, XVI (accepted manuscript, OSTI 1780888).
