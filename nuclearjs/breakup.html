Inclusive breakup

A weakly bound projectile a = b + x passes a nucleus A and only b is detected. Either both fragments fly off and A stays in its ground state (elastic breakup), or x does something to A: excites it, exchanges nucleons with it, or fuses with it (nonelastic breakup; the fusion of x alone is incomplete fusion). The sum of the two is what an experiment that counts b measures.

Nonelastic breakup in one line

d²σ/dEbdΩb|NEB = −(2/ħva) ρb(Eb) ⟨ψx|Wx|ψx⟩

ψx: the x wave left behind when b leaves with a given energy and direction; Wx = Im Ux, the absorptive part of the x + A optical potential; ρb = μbkb/((2π)³ħ²). What Wx removes from ψx is the nonelastic breakup; what ψx carries to large distances is the elastic breakup. Total inclusive = EBU + NEB. Ichimura, Austern and Vincent, Phys. Rev. C 32, 431 (1985); Lei and Moro, Phys. Rev. C 92, 044616 (2015).
